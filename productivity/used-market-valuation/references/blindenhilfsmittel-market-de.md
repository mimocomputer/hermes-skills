# German Second-Hand Market — Braille Displays / Blind Assistive Tech

Domain notes for pricing a braille display (Braillezeile), notepad, or similar Hilfsmittel. **Bands below are observations from a 2024–2026 scan of German listings and are indicative only — re-check current listings before quoting a number.**

## Market structure

- The buyer pool is small. Most devices are funded by a Kostenträger (Krankenkasse, Rentenversicherung, Integrationsamt), so private buyers usually pay out of pocket and are highly price-sensitive.
- Consequence: asking prices sit for months and get cut hard (repeated cuts of 40–70 % on one listing are normal before it moves). Treat "defensible asking price" and "what it will actually fetch" as two different numbers.

## Channels

- **pinwand-online.de** — the community board for blind and visually impaired sellers. Ads are deleted after roughly three weeks, so history only exists in the Wayback Machine (see `archived-classifieds-harvest.md`). Free to post, exactly the right audience.
- **kleinanzeigen.de** — larger reach, but braille listings are rare (a handful nationally). Worth cross-posting; search both the product name and the generic term (`Braillezeile`) plus the misspelled variants.
- **Dealer list prices** for the new-price anchor: Papenmeier Rehatechnik, COM-M, IPD, fluSoft, Help Tech, Reha-Fachhändler, medical/B2B marketplaces (medicalexpo). Manufacturer sites often publish no price at all.
- **Dealer trade-in / Ankauf** — some Reha-Händler handle used devices; ask directly rather than assuming.

## Model traps (get these right before pricing)

- Papenmeier **Braillex Live** = 40 cells **with braille input keyboard**; **Live+** = 40 cells with QWERTZ keyboard; **Live20** = 20 cells, braille input. All USB + Bluetooth, battery, ~20 h.
- Papenmeier **EL 40c / EL 80c** = USB-only stationary displays, **no** input keyboard, no Bluetooth — a different and much cheaper class than Live.
- Legacy Papenmeier 40-cell: **EL 40 P / EL 40 S / ELS 40 / EL 403**, serial or USB, long discontinued — these trade in the low hundreds.
- **Focus / Fokus** displays are Freedom Scientific, not Papenmeier; **Vario / VarioUltra / SuperVario** are VisioBraille (Baum); **Brailliant / Mantis** are HumanWare. Sellers mix these up constantly.

## Price anchors (incl. VAT, dealer list)

- 40-cell display: roughly 5,000–6,200 € new; a 40-cell **with braille input + Bluetooth + battery** sits at the top of that band (Braillex Live 40 ≈ 6,200 € at one reseller; a private seller quoted 5,200 €).
- 40-cell with QWERTZ (Mantis Q40) ≈ 2,300 € list; Brailliant BI 40X ≈ 3,700–4,000 £/$.

## Observed used asking prices (asking, not achieved)

- Near-new 40-cell **with** input/BT: ~1,500–1,800 € asking; rarely confirmed sold.
- Near-new 40-cell **USB-only** (EL 40c): 1,100–1,800 € asking, falling to ~550 € after a few months unsold.
- Legacy 40-cell (EL 40 P/S, ELS, EL 403, Braillex Trio): 300–800 € asking; a working "einwandfrei" legacy unit asked 300 €.
- 80-cell Papenmeier: 1,000–2,500 € asking, with long price-cut ladders when unsold.
- Competing-brand near-new 40-cell (e.g. Focus 40 Blue 5th, list ≈ 5,050 €): ~1,000 € asking.

## What raises / lowers the band

- **Raises:** remaining warranty from the invoice, original packaging, complete accessories (case, charger, USB cable, manual), confirmed all cells working, "Privatbesitz, nicht kassenfinanziert", insured shipping or pickup.
- **Lowers:** a successor model on the market (Papenmeier brought a newer 40-cell with braille input, which pushes used Live prices down), USB-only or no input keyboard, missing accessories/invoice, and a seller who needs the money quickly.
- Sellers conventionally exclude Sachmängelhaftung ("Privatverkauf, keine Garantie oder Rücknahme"); state the real condition honestly instead of leaning on that clause.

## Ownership caveat

If the device was funded by a Kostenträger, it may not be the user's to sell. Confirm before pricing, and let the listing say "mein Eigentum, nicht kassenfinanziert" when true.
